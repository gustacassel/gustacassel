### 🚀 What I Work On

* **Full-Stack .NET:** Web and desktop systems with ASP.NET, Blazor and Entity Framework, built around how the business actually runs.
* **ERP:** Took part in rolling out **TOTVS Protheus** during the migration from the previous ERP. Today I customize it in ADVPL / TLPP and integrate it with other systems.
* **Autodesk Ecosystem:** In-depth knowledge of **Vault PDM**, from rollout to configuration and day-to-day maintenance, plus **Inventor**, **AutoCAD** and **Factory Design Utilities**. I build add-ins for Inventor and Vault for design validation, standardization and hand-off to the ERP.
* **Performance:** Attention to memory allocation, GC behavior, multithreading and parallelism in data-heavy workloads.
* **IT Infrastructure & Networking:** Structured cabling, VLANs, video surveillance systems, hardware sizing and assembly from workstations to servers, and virtualization.
* **Microsoft 365 & Access Management:** Entra ID, SharePoint and OneDrive, with permission management, access control and governance across the company.
* **Architecture:** A pragmatic approach to Clean Code and design patterns, prioritizing maintainability without over-engineering.
